Data Analysis:
-------------
=> Data Analysis is the process of examining data to find useful information, patterns, and insights for better decision-making.
Types of Data Analysis:
----------------------
Descriptive Analysis – Tells what happened.
Example: Sales increased by 20%.
Diagnostic Analysis – Tells why it happened.
Example: Sales increased because of discounts.
Predictive Analysis – Tells what will happen.
Example: Sales may increase next month.
Prescriptive Analysis – Tells what should be done.
Example: Increase production to meet demand.
